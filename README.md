# Stonely

## Project Statment
We, The Gneiss Company™, are dedicated to making the world a little Gneiss-er, one rock at a time. The product we want to set out to create is called Stonely®. Since life is often taken for granite, Stonely® can provide users with a fun, virtual companion to benefit their day-to-day env“iron”ment. 
Stonely® gives users a rock to care for, talk to, and spend time with whenever they need a break from everyday stress. Our goal for the project is to provide a little fun and companionship for anyone wanting to take care of their own personal rock. We want a product that is easy to use, always by your side, and full of personality. Life can be a little rocky, but Stonely® will always be there. 


*Note: This project was made for my SWEN-101 class at RIT and in collaboration with Trent Kimmel and December H*
